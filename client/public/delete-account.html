Sadhana Buddy — Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Sadhana Buddy account and all associated data at any time. This page describes how to request deletion and what data is removed.

Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your spiritual practice data, journal entries, and goals will be permanently erased.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 — Within the app (recommended):
  1. Open Sadhana Buddy and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the profile icon or navigate to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
Option 2 — Email request:
  1. Send an email to privacy@sadhanabuddy.com from the email address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  3. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 30 days.

What Data Is Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account credentials (email address, display name)
  • All sadhana tracking data (japa rounds, reading pages, hearing minutes)
  • Journal entries and personal reflections
  • Spiritual practice goals and preferences
  • App activity and usage history

Data Retention

Once your deletion request is processed:

  • All personal data is deleted from our Firestore database immediately.
  • Firebase Authentication credentials are removed within 30 days.
  • No data is retained after deletion is complete.
  • Anonymized, non-identifiable aggregate statistics (if any) may be retained.
